# Community conduct

Help people understand and improve VibeScroll. Discuss code and evidence directly, respect different experience levels and leave room for disagreement.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam and deliberate disclosure of private content are unacceptable. Do not post credentials, private source material or account data. Use synthetic examples and ask before sharing another person's work.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close off-topic discussions or restrict participation. They will consider context and impact. Report conduct concerns through [the contact page](https://scroll.companynerve.com/contact); use [SECURITY](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities. Do not publish sensitive reports in issues. No response-time guarantee is offered.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests and project-maintained community spaces. It grants no authority to contact people, run promotions or access their accounts.
