# Review your first source

The maintained task-based version is [Save and inspect your first insight](help/first-useful-insight.md), rendered publicly at `/help/first-useful-insight` after release. This dated tutorial remains a compact historical reference; use current account availability and route controls.

Mode: how-to. Updated October 3, 2026. Intended for adult developers and technical founders with practical content to review. The library works before connecting a project.

1. Open [VibeScroller](https://scroll.companynerve.com/app) on phone or desktop. Sign in through Google or an email code and open Library in the default workspace.
2. Paste a permitted public link, upload permitted media or preview an Instagram Saved export. The ZIP imports links/metadata locally, without authorizing private-post access. Inspect duplicates and unavailable records.
3. Select an eligible analysis route, inspect its credit quote and content permission, then approve. Cloud analysis needs no laptop. Personal analysis needs your eligible online paired computer. Exhausted allowance stops work.
4. Open the completed source. Read main points and follow transcript/frame references. Coverage describes the sample. Reporting a creator's claim faithfully does not prove their advice true. Record an explicit useful/not-useful judgment where supported; inactivity supplies no judgment.
5. Find the source again through search, collections/topics and date filters. Export your work through Privacy. Delete sources when required; existing GitHub history does not disappear automatically.
6. Optionally connect GitHub and select repositories. Confirm profile/snapshot context. Review a proposed change, no-fit or already-implemented result. Edit/export a plan or choose no action.
7. Coding requires separate approval bound to repository, base commit, plan version, executor, funding and maximum. Review checks and patch before draft publication. You control merge/deployment. Record benefit separately from merge.

The [demo](https://scroll.companynerve.com/demo) uses labeled fixtures and causes no provider work. It explains sequence, not customer outcomes. [Capabilities](CAPABILITIES.md) lists limits and recovery. The account catalogue and published policies control billing, cancellation, refunds and retention.
