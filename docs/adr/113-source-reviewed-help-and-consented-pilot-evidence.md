# ADR 113: source-reviewed Help and consented pilot evidence

Accepted October 22, 2026 for the implemented, unreleased candidate. Exact candidate commit: `ROOT_CANDIDATE_COMMIT_PENDING`. This decision authorizes no campaign, customer claim, price activation or public release.

## Decision

Use a fixed Help manifest and approved repository Markdown as the browser documentation source. Each guide records source dependencies and review state. Unknown and draft slugs return 404, raw HTML is disabled, external URL schemes are constrained, and assets require both manifest approval and article allowlisting. Guides must remain useful without images. Source-reviewed content is not independently validated beginner guidance; capture assets need separate synthetic/privacy review before publication.

Keep a no-signup authored example with inspectable evidence and useful, already-implemented and no-fit decisions. Synthetic demo Save is usable independently of the actual viewer permission guard. Neither authored examples nor static renderer tests are evidence of model accuracy, customer outcomes or browser/device acceptance. Public pages use the existing Library theme and distinguish hosted use, open-source inspection and personal setup prerequisites.

Prepare pilot scripts, recruitment/distribution drafts and cost/cohort worksheets without sending them or manufacturing participants/results. Hacker News material contains factual product notes rather than generated submission or comment copy. Audience, existing prices, allowances and six purchase beliefs remain unchanged unless the owner separately changes them through the foundational review process.

Measure only the closed consented journey vocabulary. Citation inspection, reviewed decisions, exported plans and returns are distinct observations; deduplication must not invent repeat value. A payment observation can originate only after verified provider settlement and accepted ledger funding, with current pre-event analytics consent, an explicit measurement window and server cohort eligibility. Persist one minimal opaque-keyed observation transactionally. Duplicate, unpaid, zero-value, rejected or unconsented events do not become purchases. Refund, consent withdrawal and restore quarantine revoke unsent authority while preserving replay tombstones. No external sender is implemented.

The candidate founding-pass policy remains disabled with no active price/catalogue/checkout. Its three-year term and monthly grant calculations are policy preparation, not a sold entitlement, renewed subscription or revenue result. Provider-paid purchase evidence is also distinct from bank payout, tax/net revenue, willingness to pay and recurring usefulness.

## Evidence and limits

Targeted local Help, public-route, renderer, setup, payment/consent and policy tests support the implementation boundary. [Implementation status](../implementation-status.md) records their exact scope. Full candidate CI/build, reviewed media, independent human/device setup, live payment/provider/legal acceptance and measured comparative usefulness remain separate. The current [foundational brief](../foundational/README.md) describes this as an unreleased engineering candidate, preserving historical dated release receipts and dissent.
