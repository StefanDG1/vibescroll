# Keep the shared insight catalog proprietary and Pro-only

Mode: decision. Date: October 24, 2026, Europe/Berlin. Status: owner-selected product direction; implementation and production acceptance remain incomplete. This supersedes ADR 121's free public dataset and open-data-license direction. It preserves official user-owned Cloud investigation and the complete original launch scope.

## Access and contribution

The service-controlled shared insight database is proprietary. Only an account with current Pro entitlement may browse, search, read, save or reuse the catalog, its categories, creator views, graph, trends or catalog API/export results. There is no anonymous corpus, open dataset license, bulk public feed or free source-lookup path into these records. Existing authored software remains MIT; this decision changes dataset access, not the software license or ownership of third-party material.

All plans may contribute eligible generic results and explicitly authorized subscription capacity. Contribution does not grant Pro access. Each user keeps access to their own validly processed results and private work under existing plan rules. Capturing a matching URL alone does not make an existing catalog analysis their own result. An independently authorized processing/contribution receipt must establish that origin. Do not invent a job, infer ownership from a follow, or expose catalog existence through free cache-hit responses.

After Pro ends, already saved private copies and personal notes remain readable. Block further catalog access, new imports/reuse and catalog updates. Retained copies remain subject to required correction, safety, takedown, retention and deletion handling. Preserve personal export/removal rights; restricting bulk catalog export does not abolish access to the person's own data. Resolve exact copy/provenance behavior in the existing import lifecycle.

## Boundaries

Shared mode uses a fixed versioned generic policy. Private prompts, skills, goals, notes, repository context, evaluations, proposals, plans and customized reanalysis never enter the catalog. New eligible work requires clear contribution terms and a valid rights basis. No per-result private toggle applies within that disclosed shared mode, but a person can decline it, revoke future participation or use an eligible private route. Existing private and legacy data are not migrated.

Recurring discovery or processing needs a separate account-specific grant, creator/workload scope, model, period, concurrency and usage ceiling. Sign-in and contribution eligibility alone authorize no subscription consumption. Commercial orchestration, contributor selection and confirmed-failure handoff stay provider-gated. No credentials are pooled and no exhausted limit is bypassed.

Source/output permissions, applicable generation review, moderation, retention and removal remain required for subscriber distribution. A paywall supplies neither rights over a creator's media/ideas nor provider approval. Root prepares concrete revised contribution/access terms for operator review; no live policy bytes change in this planning update.

The seven previously reviewed README insights keep their separate exact-edition demo/save/reuse approval. They may illustrate the product under that approval. They do not open the catalog or authorize new personal generations, public copies of arbitrary Pro records or disclosure of contributor identities/follows. Unrelated private Exponential Education material stays excluded.

## Implementation and acceptance

Extend `publicArtifacts`, `publicImports`, `publicKnowledge`, source/task authority, creator watches/deliveries and persisted setup. Historical internal names need not be renamed to enforce access. Do not add a second reusable-analysis store, queue or onboarding journey. The original collections, reuse, discovery and globe requirements continue as Pro catalog capabilities, with separately approved public examples and voluntary coarse-location controls.

Root owns server-side enforcement on every read, search, graph, asset, API, import, export, background delivery and restore path. A hidden button is insufficient. Recheck entitlement at claim and result delivery and before subsequent updates. Exercise anonymous/free/Starter/Pro accounts, workspace isolation, forged and stale entitlement, cancellation during in-flight work, retained-copy reads, blocked new access/updates, raw evidence leakage, correction/removal and restore. Distinguish ownership of an original result from entitlement to other catalog results.

No runtime, billing catalog, price, release flag or deployment changes in this decision-only stage. The [current sequence](../launch-plan/2026-10-23/CLOUD-FIRST.md) retains all R/P/N acceptance and the cumulative US$2 cap. The [owner checklist](../launch-plan/2026-10-23/OWNER-CHECKLIST.md) distinguishes provider/human prerequisites from root-owned engineering.

## Offer hypothesis

Pro access to a useful, current, well-cited library is the owner-selected offer. Contribution can reduce repeated compatible analysis, but savings, coverage, paid demand and defensibility are unmeasured. A paywall cannot prevent copying visible results. Quality, provenance, coverage, freshness and useful project workflows must earn retention. No price or unlimited allowance change follows.
