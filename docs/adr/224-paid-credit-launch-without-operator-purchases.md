# ADR224: launch paid credits using existing provider funding

Date: November1,2026. Status: owner authorized; implementation in progress.

R133 supersedes the old top-up preservation requirement for new sales. The owner reports no real buyers and explicitly approves EUR17/200credits and EUR35/500credits, asks for working production checkout and forbids spending real money to test or provision it. Fresh live Stripe reads confirm zero charges, invoices and subscriptions with no additional pages. Retain audit/admin/test records; do not invent a migration or erase unknown holds.

Use the already approved Pro offer. Replace unused top-up offers, bind exact active prices and amounts to server-owned grants, and test initial/async payment, replay, refund and cancellation in the isolated Stripe sandbox. No live payment or paid model test is authorized. Checkout can be opened and inspected unpaid, then the owned session expired. Existing statutory rights and published policies remain.

User credits meter the entire quoted operation; they are not provider currency. Replace owner-only customer admission with verified paid personal Pro access, current period/wallet and risk checks. Each member funds their own work. Preserve the owner's existing policy IDs/counters and retained evidence. Public customer models are limited to supported funded Muse analysis and GLM planning; the separate owner OpenAI grant is not silently expanded.

Use a separate finite provider-credit-backed configuration and atomic shared capacity counter for new customer work. Its maximum exposure must fit verified existing balances and key limits. Unknown attempts retain their capacity. Do not purchase provider credits, enable Auto Recharge, charge the operator, pool a ChatGPT subscription or assume future subscription revenue has already replenished providers. The earlier proposed fixed USD10 monthly allowance is superseded by this prepaid-funding launch, not approved as recurring cash expenditure.

Before new sales open, verify exact live account/prices, provider configuration and remaining funded capacity, actual enabled signed webhook events including refunds/disputes, compatible publication, sandbox wallet/entitlement/processing admission, app/MCP quoting and no-charge checkout. Keep sales closed if these checks fail. Report actual live handoff separately from a paid live transaction, which is not tested.
