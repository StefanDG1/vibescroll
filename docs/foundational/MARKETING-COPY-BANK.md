# VibeScroll marketing copy bank

Working drafts for landing pages, ads, social posts, email and product demos. Started November 5, 2026. Audience for this round is founders and developers who save ideas they might use in a product.

## What to call these

**Messaging variants** is the umbrella term. A **messaging angle** is the idea a line leads with, such as the forgotten bookmark or the next feature. A **value proposition** states what someone gets. A **headline** is the main line on a page or ad. A **hook** is the opening line or visual that earns the next second of attention. A **tagline** is a short brand line meant to recur. The same wording can be a homepage headline and the hook for a demo video.

## Supported platforms for public copy

Use “public Instagram Reels and video posts, TikTok videos, and public X posts” when describing automatic analysis. Lead with what customers can do with the posts they saved.

- “Save a public Reel, TikTok video, or X post. Get cited insights you can use in your next project.”
- “Saved an idea on X? Once automatic analysis is set up, send the public post to VibeScroll in ChatGPT. VibeScroll keeps the full text and analyzes supported attachments.”

The X production check covered two text posts and two posts with one video each. Photo, GIF, and mixed-attachment cases have synthetic coverage. Keep demos and reliability claims within the formats actually checked in production. [X production evidence](../operations/evidence/launch-20261111-x-post-production.json)

**Body**

### Short demo video or social post

**Opening**

**Body**  
Here's one way to use it. Save the post in VibeScroll, pick the idea that fits your project, and review the proposal. Hand it to your coding agent to build the feature, then show the change in a pull request.

**On screen**

## First comparison to try

Compare the owner's direct product-feature line with the saved-post outcome line, the forgotten-saves hook, and the pull-request line. Keep the audience, demo and CTA the same. For a quick comprehension check, ask someone what VibeScroll helps them decide and how the plan becomes a feature or pull request. For a campaign, record the headline, placement, audience, date, qualified demo starts and resulting next steps in the idea backlog.

Lead with the full feature for a broad audience and the pull request for developers. In supporting copy, show VibeScroll's reviewable plan and the handoff to a coding agent or teammate. Use “shipped” for an example that reached users. Use “supported posts” when describing import coverage and show approval before issue creation. Base public examples on permitted content, and tie claims about speed or outcomes to measured evidence. The first-person hook works when the speaker can show their actual example.
