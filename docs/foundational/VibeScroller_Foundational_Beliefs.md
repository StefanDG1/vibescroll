They are proposed purchase conditions, not findings that every prospect already holds.

The Agora transcript argues for building a persuasive logical and emotional argument before selecting impressive words. Applied here, that means showing the source-to-project decision and the limits of the offer. It does not justify forcing a conclusion, hiding objections, inventing a proprietary mechanism, or treating certainty as evidence.

1. **I believe that some of the content I save is relevant enough to my active projects that reviewing it is worth the effort.**

   Establish this with the person's own recent source and project. Public save-and-forget comments support investigating the problem, but other commenters do not want to solve it. Casual saving is insufficient qualification.

2. **I believe that a useful summary still needs to be checked against what my project already does and what I want to change.**

   Show a source beside a recorded repository snapshot. Include an already-implemented or no-fit example. This is the proposed explanation of the missing review step, not proof that every existing reading or coding tool lacks project context.

3. **I believe that source evidence, repository evidence, and clear uncertainty help me judge whether a proposed change deserves work.**

   Demonstrate traceable main points and an inspectable rationale. The broader developer survey supports attention to verification. It does not establish VibeScroller's accuracy. A model-generated assertion without evidence cannot substantiate this belief.

4. **I believe that keeping the source, review decision, editable plan, and draft PR together could make this workflow useful beyond the tools I already own.**

   Compare the complete journey with the prospect's current stack. Readwise, SavedThat, Gemini Notebook, and GitHub agents already do meaningful parts of the work. The remaining value must be demonstrated and measured, not declared unique.

5. **I believe that I can choose the repositories, review the plan, and explicitly approve coding and its maximum cost before execution.**

   Show selected access, separate execution permission, and an actual bounded quote. The mobile web workflow must stand on its own. The optional laptop route and official personal-plan route cannot be advertised as enabled while their respective isolation or commercial-access gates remain unresolved.

6. **I believe that the enabled service and its disclosed limits are worth the price for my own recurring use after I inspect a real output.**

   Use the capped preview and clear catalogue. Explain inference funding separately from the platform price. Payment intent, repeated usefulness, and confirmed outcomes remain unknown. No earnings, fixed time savings, unlimited AI, or business benefit guarantee is needed to support an honest purchase.

The sequence should allow a prospect to decline. If their current tools work well, their sources rarely deserve action, or the enabled service does not justify its price, the offer has not earned the purchase.
