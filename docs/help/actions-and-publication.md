# Plans, coding and publication

A private project decision is a complete outcome. You may stop there or keep an editable plan.

## Before you start

Have current source evidence, confirmed project context and the appropriate role. Coding needs a verified isolated executor and a separately approved budget. An unavailable route stays blocked.

## Review each boundary

1. Inspect the suggested change and keep no-fit, deferred and already-implemented outcomes.
2. Review or edit the plan. Exporting a private plan does not authorize execution or public sharing.
3. Before coding, inspect the exact repository, base commit, plan version, allowed scope, executor, funding route, maximum spend and runtime.
4. After execution, inspect the patch and actual check results. A successful build does not establish safety or useful business outcomes.
5. Approve draft PR publication separately, only for the exact reviewed patch. A changed base, plan or permission can require a new review.
6. Review and merge through your ordinary repository process. Deployment and outcome measurement remain separate decisions.

## Public knowledge

Private/team sharing and assistant grants are not public publication authority. Only publish through an enabled explicit public-edition flow after checking rights, attribution and the material included. Do not paste private evidence URLs into public issues or launch material. Public collection and discovery availability must follow the current capability settings.

The new public-edition controls are an unreleased candidate. When available, **Load my editions and collections** first to read current status. Choose the permitted insight and rights basis, prepare the exact public preview, review its text/attribution/uncertainty and check the disclosure before **Publish reviewed edition**. A collection needs its own exact preview and review of the selected public edition versions. Changed source evidence, permission or version can invalidate an old review.

Use **Withdraw edition** or **Withdraw collection** for current publication you no longer permit. Withdrawal remains a management action even when new public publication is disabled. It removes current access; it cannot erase copies a recipient already retained.

Saving someone else's edition requires a signed-in, explicit confirmation for the reviewed slug/version. Following a link does not import it. Your own notes/corrections remain private to you; the public base can become unavailable after withdrawal, expiry or a source change. It is not a permanently available copy.

Region sharing and the globe have been retired. On a post, open **Advanced privacy and publication** to manage existing sharing. **Stop sharing my region** withdraws an earlier region choice. Public collections remain separate from your private library.

## You have finished when

You can explain which exact action you authorized, its budget, and which next decision remains yours.

## If you get stuck

Expired approval, changed repository head, unavailable isolation or revoked access must stop the dependent action. Refresh current context and review again. Do not bypass the failure by changing accounts, enabling unrestricted execution or selecting paid funding without approval.

**Tip:** Record benefit after a measurement window. A merged PR alone does not prove the idea helped.
