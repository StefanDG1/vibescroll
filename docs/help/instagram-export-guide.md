# Learn how to bring Instagram Saved links

This is the complete text alternative to the in-app export lesson. Its instructions need no animation, Instagram account or archive. The new project-builder guide requires education through its final step and explicit acknowledgement; existing libraries stay accessible. Completing this lesson is separate from requesting an export, selecting a file, importing links and receiving analyses.

Menu names below follow the export guidance reviewed for the storyboard on October 22. Meta may show Accounts Center or Meta Account, and individual labels can vary by account/device. Exact Android/iPhone screen recordings remain separate acceptance evidence. These are instructions, not a live view of your account. Use [Instagram's official export help](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/181231772500920) if your screen differs.

## 1. Find account settings

Open Instagram settings and locate Accounts Center or Meta Account. You can read the remaining steps without opening Instagram. No external page opens automatically.

## 2. Find information and export controls

Look for Your information and permissions, then Export your information. Some versions use Download your information. Do not provide an Instagram password or cookie to VibeScroll to work around a missing option.

## 3. Choose the account and destination

Select the correct Instagram profile and Export to device. A local export is not an authorization to publish its contents.

## 4. Keep the request narrow

Choose Saved when available. VibeScroll does not need messages or contacts. If Saved is not a separate category, check the actual official variant. A broader archive stays on your device; the importer reads supported Saved metadata and does not upload unrelated archive contents.

## 5. Choose compatible settings

VibeScroll recommends All time and JSON for supported Saved metadata. An export can contain links without the original media, and cannot establish retrieval rights or a complete collection history. Unknown saved dates stay unknown.

## 6. Request only if you want to

Review the selected information before using Instagram's export control. You may complete this lesson without making a request. A VibeScroll acknowledgement is not an export request sent to Meta.

## 7. Continue while Instagram prepares it

Preparation happens outside VibeScroll. Check the actual waiting state in Instagram; VibeScroll cannot verify it from your acknowledgement. A self-reported pending intention creates no inbox connection, automatic reminder or recurring monitor. You can finish the setup guide now and return later.

## 8. Keep the download private

When Instagram makes the file available, download it on your device and check its actual expiry there. Do not publish the archive or attach it to a support issue. VibeScroll has not read your notification or confirmed a download.

## 9. Preview links locally

Return to **Save** and choose the supported import option. Select the ZIP or extracted Saved JSON/HTML. The archive remains on your device. Review recognized links, duplicates and unsupported entries before sending selected metadata.

## 10. Review before committing an import

Choose the desired links and inspect the actual save/analysis choice and any processing maximum. Dismissing the file picker or failing to parse a file is not a completed import. Re-importing the same supported metadata should report existing sources. Saved links can remain inaccessible or waiting rather than generating invented media evidence.

In the in-app lesson, reach this final step and choose **I've seen how it works**. Reading, static controls and reduced-motion mode are equivalent educational paths; no timer or animation playback requirement determines completion.

After acknowledgement, you can record that you requested an export, open Save if you already have a download, or choose **Do this later**. A recorded request is self-reported. It does not prove Meta accepted it, import anything, spend credits or verify a result. [Import deliberately](/help/imports-and-data) when the real file is available.
