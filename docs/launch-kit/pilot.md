# Run a small useful-output pilot

Status: script prepared, no invitations sent or sessions completed.

## Qualify twenty prospects privately

Use opaque IDs in the shared worksheet. Keep names, addresses and relationship notes in protected private storage.

For each prospect record whether they own an active project, save practical sources, can supply three permitted sources, use a relevant current workaround, can give informed participation consent, and have an eligible local or explicitly selected cloud route. Record why a person declines. Do not infer demographics or willingness to pay.

Invite ten qualified people. Run five observed sessions, repair repeated failures, then run five independent sessions. Agree permission and a bounded processing maximum before any live task. Another account operated by the founder is not independent acceptance.

## Invitation draft, unsent

I am testing VibeScroll, a library for saved ideas and source-backed project decisions. Would you try it with three sources you are allowed to process and one project? I want to observe what helps, what is confusing and whether a no-fit answer is useful. Processing availability and any cost will be reviewed before work. Participation does not require a purchase. Please do not send private source material by email.

## Observed session

1. Ask the participant to show a recent practical save and explain where it went and what happened next. Do this before demonstrating VibeScroll.
2. Explain voluntary participation, privacy, what will be recorded and how to stop. Avoid recording private screens by default.
3. Ask them to explain the public example's benefit and limits. Record their own words and wrong turns, without coaching first.
4. Have them save one permitted source, select a usable route and explain the maximum, waiting state and provider allowance.
5. Ask them to open a ready result, inspect a citation and name an omission or uncertainty.
6. Ask them to find the result again and correct its filing.
7. Optionally connect a selected project, confirm current context and inspect useful/no-fit/already-implemented outcomes.
8. Ask them to retain/export a private plan if useful. Have them explain what remains unauthorized.
9. Ask whether this improves their current method, what they would stop using, and whether the current monthly offer is worth purchasing. Record an actual purchase or decline separately from an intention.
10. Agree an observation window for return use. Follow-up messaging requires separate authorization.

Record assistance, interruptions, elapsed task time, completion, safe error code, correction needed and route cost. A person who finishes only after developer intervention did not complete independently.

## Independent sessions

Give the maintained public Help and the selected permitted route. Do not add private founder instructions that conceal missing setup. Record support requested and unsuccessful attempts. Four of five independent completions is a proposed signal for another small cohort, not a market benchmark.

## Correction and decline log

For each correction record opaque participant/session ID, task, observed failure, severity, reproduction, fix, verification and remaining uncertainty. Do not copy private claims into analytics.

For a decline record the participant's stated reason, current alternative and whether the obstacle concerns usefulness, trust, setup, cost, source access or timing. Preserve contrary evidence.

## Measurement definitions

- First useful output: participant identifies a ready result as useful within the session window, out of all participants who attempted first source.
- Evidence inspection: participant opens or follows a supporting reference, out of ready-result viewers.
- Decision reviewed: participant explains a fit outcome using current context, out of participants attempting project review.
- Plan retained/exported: confirmed save/export action, out of participants choosing planning.
- Return use: another deliberate library task in the agreed later window, out of participants eligible to return.
- Purchase: verified successful payment, out of participants who received the tested offer. Checkout opening or payment intent does not count.
- Cost: estimates, reservations, unresolved holds and settled provider/platform charges remain separate.

Optional analytics needs consent. No source text, project contents, complete URL, account email or session replay. This worksheet is manual evidence, not a claim that all events already exist in the application.
