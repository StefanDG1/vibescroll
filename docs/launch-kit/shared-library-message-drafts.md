## Proposed central message

Make scrolling useful.

A Pro library of cited ideas. Personal next steps for what you are building.

The product direction: general insights from eligible public posts contribute to a proprietary reusable catalog. Only Pro can browse or newly save/reuse that catalog. All plans may contribute, without gaining catalog access. When a compatible analysis already exists, eligible Pro readers can use it without starting the same general analysis again. People retain access to their own validly processed results under existing plan rules. Goals, personal notes, repository reviews, proposals and plans remain private.

## Proposed explanation after verified release

Connect your own eligible ChatGPT account through the official cloud host. Send a permitted post link or follow a supported creator. Read the general ideas beside their source evidence, then decide what is useful to you.

The standard general-analysis mode contributes eligible results to the proprietary Pro catalog. Private advanced reanalysis is separate. Explain this before processing; never bury subscriber distribution or recurring subscription-contribution authority in a general sign-in.

One compatible general analysis can serve several readers. That is the proposed reuse mechanism, not a guarantee of reduced subscription bills or unlimited capacity. Source access, coverage, account allowance and current permissions still apply. Attribution and reporting/removal controls accompany catalog source records. After Pro ends, saved private copies and personal notes remain readable; further catalog access, new reuse and updates stop. Required removal/correction handling still applies.

## Claims requiring evidence

Do not publish "instant," "fully analyzes every video," "works with all TikTok/Instagram links," "free unlimited AI," "no server costs," "everyone saves money," "the best creators," or invented usage/customer counts.

A graph can describe the sources represented in the library. Trend indicators need an observation period, source coverage and denominator. They do not represent the whole platform. Creator usefulness should reflect permitted recorded judgments and their limits, not a popularity ranking presented as truth.

An open-source software license is separate from permission to process, publish or relicense a creator's media. Review and disclose the proprietary contribution/access terms before catalog release. Do not offer an open dataset, anonymous corpus, bulk public feed or free catalog bypass. Personal exports remain available under their own rights. Source video, transcript and frame rights do not transfer automatically.

## Future direction

Other provider subscriptions, editing-style assistance and similar-video ideas remain roadmap concepts. No automatic imitation, creator impersonation, raw-media redistribution or cross-provider entitlement is advertised.

Paid demand, contribution willingness and database defensibility need observation. A paywall does not prevent copying visible results. Do not advertise a guaranteed moat, achieved savings or customer retention. No price, live billing or software-license change follows from this draft.
