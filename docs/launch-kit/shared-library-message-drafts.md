Mode: reference. Unsent concepts, October 23, 2026. Read [F044](../foundational/idea-backlog.md#f044-codex-cloud-first-and-a-public-insight-library-october-23) and the [Cloud-first release gates](../launch-plan/2026-10-23/CLOUD-FIRST.md). These are copy hypotheses, not statements that the new route or public library is available.

## Proposed central message

Make scrolling useful.

A shared library of cited ideas. Personal next steps for what you are building.

The product direction: general insights from eligible public posts contribute to a reusable library. When a compatible analysis already exists, people can use it without starting the same general analysis again. Goals, personal notes, repository reviews, proposals and plans remain private.

## Proposed explanation after verified release

Connect your own eligible ChatGPT account through the official cloud host. Send a permitted post link or follow a supported creator. Read the general ideas beside their source evidence, then decide what is useful to you.

The standard general-analysis mode contributes eligible results to the public library. Private advanced reanalysis is separate. Explain this before processing; never bury publication or subscription-contribution authority in a general sign-in.

One compatible general analysis can serve several readers. That is the proposed reuse mechanism, not a guarantee of reduced subscription bills or unlimited capacity. Source access, coverage, account allowance and current permissions still apply. Attribution and reporting/removal controls accompany every public source.

## Claims requiring evidence

Do not publish "instant," "fully analyzes every video," "works with all TikTok/Instagram links," "free unlimited AI," "no server costs," "everyone saves money," "the best creators," or invented usage/customer counts.

A graph can describe the sources represented in the library. Trend indicators need an observation period, source coverage and denominator. They do not represent the whole platform. Creator usefulness should reflect permitted recorded judgments and their limits, not a popularity ranking presented as truth.

An open-source software license is separate from permission to process, publish or relicense a creator's media. Choose and disclose the public insight/data license before making bulk-reuse promises. Source video, transcript and frame rights do not transfer automatically.

## Future direction

Other provider subscriptions, editing-style assistance and similar-video ideas remain roadmap concepts. No automatic imitation, creator impersonation, raw-media redistribution or cross-provider entitlement is advertised.
