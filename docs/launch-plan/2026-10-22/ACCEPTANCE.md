## Five separate states

- **Engineered:** the relevant code, persistence, authority checks, controls and documentation exist. A signing key, provider permission, customer or human observation cannot be manufactured to complete this state. Final media deliverables are still part of engineering/presentation scope, not silently waived.
- **Locally tested:** the identified commit passed the recorded local checks. Synthetic fixtures, mocked providers, native imports, static rendering and local browser review keep their actual labels. A serial pass does not erase a parallel timeout or certify a later head.
- **Live integration verified:** an authorized actual account/provider/runtime journey passed with persisted receipts at the identified deployed version. Existing production observations do not verify a new gated path.
- **Independently usable:** another qualified person can complete the relevant task on their own account/device and explain evidence, limits, cost and permissions. Two founder-controlled accounts, a High code review or a screenshot are not this acceptance.
- **Released:** the exact approved commit/artifact has its required CI, publication/deployment and post-release verification. An open draft PR, successful build or local signed-manifest fixture is not a released product or signed installer.

States apply to the October 22 additions. Dated historical V1 receipts in [implementation status](../../implementation-status.md) remain valid at their recorded scope, but are not copied into these columns as new passes.
