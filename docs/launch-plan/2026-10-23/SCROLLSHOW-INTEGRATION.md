## Commercial migration acceptance beyond marketing copy

The selected future presentation is Pro EUR 35/month, EUR 350/year and EUR 800 once for ongoing access. Every choice includes a defined monthly AI allowance. EUR 350 is EUR 70 less than twelve EUR 35 payments for equivalent service. EUR 800 has no selected fixed monthly equivalent or fixed three-year term. Do not use "unlimited," "most popular," unsupported scarcity, invented savings or perpetual operation.

Before implementing sale activation, root prepares:

1. A versioned catalog proposal with all three payment purposes mapped to Pro, legacy price/product mapping, existing paid periods, limits and preserved credit balances. Keep free/legacy contribution and own-result access. New offer presentation does not erase existing plans.
2. Exact allowance amount/unit, eligible workloads, service funding, account-specific subscription authority, renewal dates, rollover, exhaustion and failed/unknown-attempt treatment. Do not assume the current six-hundred-credit grant or equate a user's provider quota with a company-funded guarantee.
3. Fully consumed usage, storage, support, fees/tax/refunds and long-retention scenarios, including low new-sales conditions. Define ongoing access/service scope, material change, discontinuation/remedies and any explicit minimum commitment. Keep published policy bytes immutable until the exact revised release is reviewed.

An idempotent one-time Pro purchase lifecycle, distinct from credits/top-ups; verified webhook/order/workspace ownership; refund/dispute/reversal reconciliation; recurring allowance issuance independent of subscription invoice events; renewal job deduplication; bounded catch-up; no payment from mere redirect success.

Legacy-to-new, canceled/expired subscription, ongoing purchase, refund/dispute, duplicate/out-of-order webhook, stale entitlement, monthly renewal, source-job in-flight, catalog update, retained-copy and restore tests. Verify no double grants, silent conversion, balance loss, Pro leak or fabricated subscription.
