# Laytuh competitor review

Mode: research reference. Checked October 20, 2026. Requested by the owner.

Laytuh competes for capturing, organizing and revisiting saved social content. Its positioning emphasizes everyday activities and reminders. VibeScroll's candidate distinction is source analysis connected to project decisions and reviewed implementation. This is an assessment, not a demonstrated quality advantage.

## Product, audience and availability

Wingbie Inc. publishes Laytuh as a consumer productivity app. The homepage addresses forgetting to use saved content, with examples involving meals, trips, gifts, exercise and shopping. It advertises share-menu capture from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, WhatsApp, X and browsers, followed by automatic filing and reminders. These are input-channel claims, not verified video-download coverage. [Homepage](https://laytuh.com/)

Store descriptions include collaborative boards and polls. Apple showed an English iPhone app requiring iOS 18 or later, free download and version 1.0.1 dated October 10. Its notes mention an AI permission choice and share-menu guidance. Google Play showed an October 10 update, a 100+ download band, caption display and retry controls. Apple had insufficient ratings for an overview. These snapshots do not establish retention. [Apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/laytuh/id6816296910), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.laytuh.app)

## Processing, privacy and price

The privacy policy describes on-device screenshot OCR and link/caption classification, with server AI classification when needed. It also describes Ask over personal saves and an optional agent. Providers include Supabase, Cloudflare Workers AI, Google Places and platform oEmbed services. Vaults are private by default; published boards, polls and creator pages can be public. Optional app analytics/crash reporting are on by default; website measurement requires cookie acceptance. These are disclosures, not an independent audit. [Privacy](https://laytuh.com/privacy)

The terms describe keeping links and previews, with platform-hosted embeds. Saving is stated to remain free; paid extras may be added. The optional agent requires approval, includes some preview actions and does not book or buy. No paid checkout was verified. [Terms](https://laytuh.com/terms)

## Evidence limits

- Broad capture does not establish audiovisual understanding. The reviewed sources do not verify video decoding, transcription, sampled-frame reasoning, timestamped evidence or private-post access. Absence of evidence does not prove those capabilities are absent. [Privacy](https://laytuh.com/privacy), [terms](https://laytuh.com/terms)
- The homepage illustrates nearby/travel reminders, but the privacy policy says precise location is not collected and nearby reminders would require a future opt-in. Location-triggered availability remains unresolved. [Homepage](https://laytuh.com/), [privacy](https://laytuh.com/privacy)
- The homepage displays 4,800+ users. Google Play's download band covers one channel; these different populations cannot validate or disprove each other. Active users, revenue and retention were not independently verified. [Homepage](https://laytuh.com/), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.laytuh.app)

## Recommendations and open questions

Research recommendations only; no scope or pricing change is approved:

1. Distinguish storing a reference, reading captions and analyzing audio/visual content in intake/status wording. Broad capture could be supported separately from analysis.
2. Test returning an applicable source at the right moment. Laytuh challenges the assumption that organization alone brings people back.
3. Compare evidence, project relevance and reviewed execution. Avoid saying every competitor merely stores bookmarks or only VibeScroll turns saves into action.
4. In a later authorized hands-on comparison, use identical permitted public posts, including caption-poor and visual-only instructions. Record capture steps, retained content, classification, Ask/search accuracy, reminders, failures and deletion/export. Mark inaccessible tests skipped.

Unresolved: Facebook/X/WhatsApp payload handling, private links, bulk Instagram export import, audiovisual depth, Ask limits/quality, reminder reliability and independent demand. No account access, purchase, implementation or customer-facing claim change follows automatically from this note.
