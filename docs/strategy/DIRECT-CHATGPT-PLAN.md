## Supported protocol and measurement

Follow the current [inference guide](https://developers.openai.com/siwc/token-sharing-open-source/models-and-inference) and [preview limitations](https://developers.openai.com/siwc/token-sharing-open-source/preview-limitations). The published contract accepts prepared text and images, not transcription/audio/video endpoints. Do not reuse the paid OpenAI adapter, its forbidden request fields, or undocumented ChatGPT endpoints. Bound input, stream bytes and execution time; do not describe a client timeout as confirmed provider cancellation. Model availability and reasoning capability must come from the authorized account's supported contract.

The [token reference](https://developers.openai.com/siwc/token-sharing-open-source/token-reference) documents one-hour access credentials and rotating 30-day refresh credentials for the public flow. Reconfirm the approved hosted contract rather than assuming identical terms. A refresh can normally avoid another sign-in, but it cannot override revocation or expiry.

Measure Save acknowledgement, metadata, worker preparation, ASR, analysis dispatch, first output, confirmed completion, validation, persistence and shared association separately. The accepted native runs included about55–63 seconds of event wait; removing that wait is an engineering expectation, not a measured direct-route speedup or inference SLA. Compare the same retained evidence before a fresh-link test.
