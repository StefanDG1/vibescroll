# Content rights and copyright reports

Status: published. Effective 3 October 2026. Version: 1.0.0. Release: v1-2026-10-03.1.

VibeScroller processes content for the user's private workspace. A public URL does not automatically establish permission to download, retain, redistribute, or reuse that content. Users must have an appropriate basis for their requested processing and must not bypass access restrictions.

The service does not provide a public archive of imported creator videos. Original media is temporary by default. Source links and limited evidence remain private under the retention policy. Cross-customer reuse of processed sources is disabled in V1.

To report a rights concern, email contact@exponentialeducation.ro with the work or right involved, the affected VibeScroller location or identifier if available, your contact details, and a clear explanation of your authority and concern. Do not send sensitive identity documents unless specifically required through a secure process.

We assess reports, may restrict the affected content while investigating, and provide an appropriate response route. We can request necessary additional information. We do not promise that every unverified allegation results in permanent removal, or that a private customer library is immune from a valid rights claim.

Removal and restriction propagate to retained media, transcripts, evidence, caches, and search records where required. We do not use a cache to bypass a valid removal. A separately published GitHub PR may require action on GitHub, and we will explain that boundary.

The owner reports permission to use the Mark Builds Brands resources. The product's permission record must preserve that statement and any available supporting evidence. It must not imply endorsement or grant rights over unrelated third-party content embedded in those resources.
