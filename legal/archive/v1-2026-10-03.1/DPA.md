# Data processing agreement

Status: published. Effective 3 October 2026. Version: 1.0.0. Release: v1-2026-10-03.1. Applies to customer instruction data when accepted with the service terms; publication alone does not execute an agreement.

## Parties and instructions

The customer identified in the service order acts as controller for personal data it submits for processing on its instructions. EXPONENTIAL EDUCATION S.R.L. acts as processor for that processing. The operator remains controller for its own account administration, billing, security, and legal obligations as explained in the privacy notice.

The subject is processing content and selected repository materials to provide the contracted VibeScroller service. Processing lasts for the service term and the documented deletion period. Operations include collection, temporary storage, transcription, analysis, retrieval, proposal generation, approved code execution, support, export, and deletion.

Data can include customer users' identifiers and contact details, source creators' names, images and voices, and personal data within customer-provided content or repository files. The service is not designed for bulk sensitive personal data, criminal records, or unrelated regulated records. The customer must not submit them unless a separate suitable agreement and safeguards exist.

The customer's configuration, documented actions, approved jobs, and this agreement form its instructions. The processor will inform the customer if an instruction appears to conflict with applicable data-protection law and will not use a model output as a new instruction from the customer.

## Processor obligations

The processor limits access to authorized personnel bound by confidentiality. It applies the technical and organizational measures described in the security schedule, including tenant isolation, protected credentials, private objects, restricted execution, access logging, retention controls, and incident response.

The processor does not use customer instruction data for cross-customer model training by default. It does not sell that data. It does not publish it as marketing content without separate authorization.

The processor assists the customer, taking account of the processing and information available, with rights requests, security obligations, incident assessment, and legally required impact assessments or consultations. Assistance beyond the ordinary service can require a reasonable separate arrangement that does not prevent compliance with mandatory duties.

## Subprocessors and transfers

The customer authorizes the listed subprocessors for the contracted route. The live register identifies legal entities, service purpose, processing location information, and applicable safeguards. A draft provider list is not evidence that contracts have been signed.

The processor provides advance notice of a material new subprocessor, normally at least 30 days where practicable. The customer may raise a substantiated data-protection objection. The parties seek a reasonable alternative. If no suitable alternative exists, the customer can stop the affected service and receive any remedy required by law or the applicable order.

An urgent security replacement may require shorter notice. The processor informs the customer promptly and preserves the same protection obligations. International transfers require an applicable lawful mechanism and assessment, not merely selection of an EU storage bucket.

## Incidents

The processor notifies the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting the customer's instruction data. The notice includes the information available about the incident, likely effects, containment, and contact route. Information can follow in stages as the investigation develops.

The processor preserves relevant evidence proportionately and cooperates with the customer's assessment. This agreement does not replace the customer's own notification duties or authorize unnecessary retention of unrelated source content.

## Return, deletion, and audit

At the end of service, the customer can export supported data and request deletion. Active-system and backup deletion follow the documented schedule, except where law requires retention. The processor retains only the data needed for that duty and protects it from unrelated use.

The processor provides information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its obligations. The parties arrange proportionate audits or reviews that protect other customers' data and security. Existing independent reports can support the review but do not automatically replace legally required audit rights.

## Schedule of measures

The service enforces workspace membership and role checks on private queries and actions. Private evidence uses authenticated, short-lived access and private object storage. Provider credentials are encrypted and are excluded from customer coding tasks. Approved cloud media and coding jobs run in bounded ephemeral microVMs, with restricted network access, cancellation and teardown controls. Coding requires separate approval of the plan, repository, base commit, funding and maximum spend before a patch can be published.

Source and account deletion revoke access, fence delayed results and apply deletion records during backup recovery. Temporary media and retained data follow the [privacy notice](PRIVACY.md). Independently encrypted backups have bounded retention; a restored environment remains locked while later deletions and restored credentials are reconciled. Optional analytics requires separate consent and excludes source content and repository material. These measures describe the deployed controls; they do not claim certification, exhaustive threat coverage or professional review.

This agreement supplements the service terms and prevails for processor obligations where they conflict. Mandatory data-protection law prevails over inconsistent contractual wording.
