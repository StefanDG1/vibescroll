# Publish and enforce the policies

Mode: how-to. These drafts are not legal advice or evidence of legal approval.

## Prepare publication

1. Confirm the operator details against the current company record.
2. Record the actual domestic VAT treatment and any separate special registration.
3. Verify the markets enabled for checkout and the applicable invoice workflow.
4. Select the active providers and record their contracts, roles, retention, regions, and transfer safeguards.
5. Compare every policy statement with the deployed implementation.
6. Review consumer terms, refund handling, copyright processing, and applicable AI transparency requirements with qualified advice.
7. Set the effective date, policy version, and publication approval record.
8. Publish the policies and retain the previous versions.

Do not remove a draft label merely because a page builds. Do not publish the inactive candidate provider list as if every agreement exists.

## Bind policy to behavior

| Policy statement | Required implementation |
| --- | --- |
| No silent paid fallback | Funding-route lock and explicit reapproval test |
| Temporary originals | Storage lifecycle plus cleanup sweeper and deletion receipts |
| Private library | Tenant-scoped queries, private bucket, no indexed private routes |
| Easy cancellation | Authenticated cancellation action and emailed confirmation |
| First 14-day refund policy | Invoice-aware refund workflow covering eligible weekly renewals |
| Separate optional consent | Independent stored consent choices, no bundled prechecked box |
| Provider disconnection | Credential revocation, job cancellation, clear retained-data behavior |
| No cross-customer training | Disabled data export/training routes and tested privacy defaults |
| Account export and deletion | Bounded export job, access control, deletion tombstones, backup treatment |
| Rights requests | Support workflow, identity verification, deadlines, and audit record |
| AI transparency | Labels, output provenance, and deployment-specific legal assessment |

## Implement checkout consent

Show the complete payable amount, billing interval, renewal behavior, included allowance, credit expiry, and relevant tax treatment before payment. Link the terms and refund policy.

Record acceptance of the service terms and any explicit request for immediate service. Keep optional marketing consent separate and off by default. Do not label a blanket withdrawal waiver as mandatory for all service subscriptions.

Send a durable confirmation with plan, interval, price, applicable policy versions, cancellation route, and withdrawal information. The operator must be able to reproduce the version accepted for an invoice.

## Test legal routes

Check every policy route, footer link, mobile layout, contact channel, withdrawal request, cancellation path, export, and deletion workflow. Verify that no template company identity, example email, placeholder domain, or unused provider appears in the public copy.

Keep a release record for legal and tax evidence. The implementation agent can prepare the configuration and test it, but cannot create an official registration or claim professional review occurred.
