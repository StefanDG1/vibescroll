## Data we process

We process account identifiers, email address, workspace membership, preferences, policy choices, billing details and transaction references, support communications, and limited technical logs.

When you submit content, we process its URL or file, available metadata, audio, selected frames, transcript, summaries, insights, and your review decisions. Content may contain other people's voices, images, or names. Do not submit material you are not entitled to process.

When you connect a repository, we process the selected repository's identity, permitted code and documentation, selected commit, business profile, proposals, plans, patches, test results, and PR state. We avoid unrelated files and exclude known secret locations.

A local runner sends its device identity, supported capabilities, job status, authorized artifacts, and redacted diagnostics. We do not need your local Codex OAuth token. A local task can still send relevant repository context to its selected AI provider.

## Purposes and legal bases

We use necessary account and service data to perform our contract with you. We process legally required billing and tax records to comply with legal duties. We use proportionate security, abuse-prevention, and service-integrity data for legitimate interests, subject to the applicable balancing assessment and your rights.

Optional marketing, nonessential tracking, and any separately proposed use requiring consent depend on the relevant choice. Refusing optional consent does not prevent use of the paid core service. You can withdraw consent as easily as you give it.

Workspace category names and source assignments are private. If a workspace owner or admin explicitly suggests a shared category, we receive that name for review. An approved name and its aliases may be used in the app-wide vocabulary. We do not share the linked videos, transcripts, frames, insights or repository material through that process. Do not submit confidential or personal information in a shared name.

We do not use private source content or repository material to train models across customers by default. We do not sell your private library or use it as a public SEO archive.

Optional product analytics uses PostHog EU Cloud only after analytics consent. We send approved event names and coarse properties such as processing state, route and capped counts. We exclude source URLs, captions, transcripts, evidence, repository names and files, account emails and model prompts. The provider still receives network metadata needed to receive a request. We disable replay and automatic capture. Rejecting analytics leaves the application usable. The cookie preferences control lets you withdraw permission for future events; it cannot retract events already sent. See [the cookie policy](COOKIES.md).

Local subscription credentials remain on that laptop. The computer must stay connected while processing. Telegram capture is deferred and is not active in this deployment.

## Retention

Temporary original media, failed uploads, extracted audio, and redundant frames normally expire within 24 hours of completed processing or terminal failure. Selected evidence, transcripts, summaries, and insights remain while you keep them in an entitled workspace.

Intermediate caches expire within seven days. Raw repository snapshots normally expire within 24 hours after the relevant operation. Redacted run diagnostics normally remain for 14 days, and minimal security audit records for 90 days.

When you delete content, we remove normal access immediately and target deletion from active product systems within seven days. Backups normally expire within 30 days. We use deletion records to prevent deleted content from reappearing during restoration. Lawful holds can require limited exceptions.

Accounting records follow the applicable statutory schedule. The initial Romanian accounting policy uses the period required by the current accounting rules, distinct from any additional OSS or dispute requirement. We retain only the data needed for that duty. Deleting your source library does not delete a legally required invoice.

Explicitly authorized company invoice operators can download buyer details and payment-provider invoice records for the accountant's manual invoicing workflow. Customer workspace ownership alone does not grant that permission. Source media, transcripts and repository content are excluded from these accounting downloads.

The live notice must state any materially different provider retention that applies to the enabled service. No provider is assumed to follow our own cache timing automatically.

## Your choices and rights

You can view and correct supported account data, change notification and optional tracking choices, export your library, disconnect providers, and request account deletion. Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing.

Contact us for rights that the interface cannot complete. We verify the request proportionately and respond within the applicable legal timeframe. You can complain to the Romanian supervisory authority or another competent data-protection authority. You do not have to complain to us first.

Disconnecting GitHub stops further authorized access. It does not erase a PR already published to GitHub. That platform's records remain subject to its own controls and your permissions there.
