# Cancellation, withdrawal, and refunds

Status: published. Effective 3 October 2026. Version: 1.0.0. Release: v1-2026-10-03.1.

## Cancel renewal

Cancel in the application's billing settings. You can also contact contact@exponentialeducation.ro when the account interface is unavailable. We do not require a sales call or a retention questionnaire.

Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the paid period. Weekly means seven days, not a calendar week. The confirmation states the final access date and whether another payment is scheduled. Keep the confirmation in your billing history.

## Merchant payment support

The checkout and receipt identify the merchant for your transaction. For Stripe Managed Payments purchases, Link provides transaction invoices, order management and payment support. You may use the receipt’s merchant support link or contact VibeScroller with an order reference for help with the service or a refund request. We coordinate the request with the applicable payment route; you do not need to determine the technical provider responsible before reporting a problem.

Cancellation in VibeScroller billing settings remains available. Merchant terms do not replace the service commitments or mandatory rights described here. A provider sandbox does not expose every live Link order-management feature, so that limitation must be recorded before release rather than represented as a completed live purchase test.

## Initial refund policy

For an individual's first subscription, we offer a refund of subscription charges made during the first 14 days when the request is made within that period. This includes weekly renewals charged inside that initial window. This voluntary policy does not reduce any statutory right, including rights that apply in other circumstances.

When you request immediate service, checkout records that request separately. We do not claim that opening the app or consuming one credit automatically waives every consumer withdrawal right. Any legally permitted deduction or exception outside our voluntary policy requires the appropriate information and consent.

We process an approved refund to the original payment method where possible. We initiate it within the applicable legal deadline. The payment provider or bank can take additional time to display it. We send confirmation and do not require you to accept platform credits instead of a legally required money refund.

## Extra credits and failed work

Unused purchased extra credits are tracked separately from included allowance. Contact us to request a refund of unused purchased credits where the policy or law permits. A refund calculation identifies the relevant purchase and consumed service. It never treats included promotional credits as cash.

A reservation for work that never starts is released. For a technical failure within our service that produces no usable billed result, restore the relevant platform credits. If you paid an external API provider directly, we cannot reverse that provider's invoice, but we will supply the relevant job record and support information.

We do not charge you for an automatic switch to a provider you did not approve. No such switch is permitted by the product specification.

## Faulty or nonconforming service

The initial refund window does not replace remedies for faulty, unavailable, or nonconforming service. Mandatory consumer remedies remain available. Contact us with the account or invoice reference and a description of the problem. Do not include card numbers or API keys.

Business customers' additional commercial terms can be stated in their signed order. A business label cannot be used to remove consumer rights from a person who is legally acting as a consumer.

## Withdrawal form

You may use this wording, but it is not mandatory:

"To EXPONENTIAL EDUCATION S.R.L., contact@exponentialeducation.ro: I give notice that I withdraw from my VibeScroller service contract. Account email: ____. Order or invoice reference: ____. Date ordered: ____. Name: ____. Date: ____."

Only request information needed to identify and process the withdrawal. A typed online notice does not require a handwritten signature.
