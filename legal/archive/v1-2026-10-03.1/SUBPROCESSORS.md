# Provider and subprocessor register

Status: published. Effective 3 October 2026. Version: 1.0.0. Release: v1-2026-10-03.1.

EXPONENTIAL EDUCATION S.R.L. operates VibeScroller. This register describes configured production services and customer-selected integrations. A provider can process service content on instructions and independently control account, fraud or statutory records. The applicable agreement determines its role.

## Customer-connected services and backups

GitHub receives selected repository reads, authorized patches and PR metadata under customer permissions. GitHub Inc. or GitHub B.V. acts according to its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and applicable account agreement. An Enterprise DPA is not assumed to apply to a personal account. Separately, the operator's restricted backup workflow stores encrypted database/marker archives and encrypted retained evidence in seven-day GitHub artifacts. Other customers cannot access those artifacts.

OpenAI is optional and customer-controlled. The enabled personal route transcribes audio on the paired laptop and sends selected frames, transcript and relevant context through the customer's supported ChatGPT-plan connection. Subscription credentials remain on that laptop. The optional API route uses the customer's selected account. The [OpenAI privacy terms](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and account settings apply. The operator does not pool a personal subscription across customers; general hosted subscription inference remains disabled pending its applicable commercial contract.

Telegram and Gemini subscription connections are deferred and inactive. Netlify and E2B are retired. Historical tests do not make them current processors.

## Retention and changes

Our [privacy notice](PRIVACY.md) describes product retention and deletion. Those timers do not automatically erase authentication, email, payment, AI abuse-monitoring, support or legally retained provider records. We record material changes and support objections where the applicable processing agreement requires them.

Agreement evidence and publication approval are tracked separately. This draft does not claim professional legal review, blanket EU-only processing or global model-training permission.
